EconML: Economics for Machine Learning

NeurIPS'26 Workshop
Atlanta, December 12 or 13, 2026

About

EconML brings together researchers from machine learning, economics, game theory, and related fields to map the economic consequences of ML's growing success, anticipate emerging risks, and develop economic interventions that support long-term sustainability and benefit. The workshop bridges micro-scale mechanism design and market-level analysis, and we welcome contributions that connect economic ideas to core questions in theoretical and applied machine learning.

Research Themes

The workshop is organized around two complementary ways that economics and machine learning can inform one another.

Theme 1

Economic Tools for ML

How can mechanism design, social choice, contract theory, and behavioral economics improve the way models are trained, evaluated, and aligned?

  • Preference aggregation and social choice for alignment
  • Pricing of data, feedback, training, and inference
  • Incentive-aware evaluation and strategic classification
  • Mechanisms for eliciting high-quality data and feedback
  • Behavioral and formal models of AI decision-making
Theme 2

ML Ecosystems with Interacting Models

What changes when models, providers, users, and intermediaries adapt to one another in shared environments?

  • Competition, market concentration, and model multiplicity
  • AI supply chains and their downstream effects
  • Algorithmic collusion and multi-agent learning
  • Feedback loops and performative prediction
  • Ecosystem-level incentive design and social welfare
